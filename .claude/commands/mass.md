# Mass

Run `npm run fleet -- mass` (add `--json` if you need to work with the rows). Explain the categories: under 5% over is minor risk, 5% to under 20% substantial, 20% or more severe (HVNL ss 98 to 100). Ask what the loader and consignor were told: they share the duty.
